Buckling of rectangular plates (Kassapoglou)#

The functions of composites.kassapoglou give closed-form and two-term Rayleigh-Ritz estimates of the buckling loads of flat rectangular laminated plates and of the effective width of a post-buckled plate, following Kassapoglou [1], chapters 6 and 7. They are preliminary-design tools: they take the bending stiffnesses \(D_{ij}\) of the CLPT, e.g. lam.D11 of a Laminate, assume simply supported edges and neglect the transverse shear deformation, see Laminate stiffness and plate theories. The loads are resultants per unit length, positive in compression for the returned critical loads.

Energy and assumptions#

For a deflection \(w(x, y)\) of a plate of length \(a\) along \(x\) and width \(b\) along \(y\), under the in-plane resultants \(N_x\), \(N_{xy}\), positive in tension, the total potential energy is (Kassapoglou [1], Eq. 6.32, here with \(D_{16}\) and \(D_{26}\)):

(1)#\[\Pi = \int_0^a \int_0^b \left[ \frac{1}{2} \left( D_{11} w_{,xx}^2 + 2 D_{12} w_{,xx} w_{,yy} + D_{22} w_{,yy}^2 + 4 D_{66} w_{,xy}^2 + 4 D_{16} w_{,xx} w_{,xy} + 4 D_{26} w_{,yy} w_{,xy} \right) + \frac{1}{2} N_x w_{,x}^2 + N_{xy} w_{,x} w_{,y} \right] dx \, dy\]

With a symmetric laminate, \(B = 0\), the bending is decoupled from the membrane state, which is uniform; the methods below assume this. The bending-twisting terms \(D_{16}\), \(D_{26}\) are neglected by all functions but calc_Nxx_crit_combined_shear_full().

Uniaxial compression#

With the mode \(w = \sin(m \pi x/a) \sin(n \pi y/b)\) of a specially orthotropic plate, \(D_{16} = D_{26} = 0\), simply supported on four edges, the buckling load is exact (Kassapoglou [1], Eq. 6.6, p. 129), with \(AR = a/b\):

(2)#\[N_{xx,crit} = \frac{\pi^2}{a^2 m^2} \left( D_{11} m^4 + 2 (D_{12} + 2 D_{66}) m^2 n^2 AR^2 + D_{22} n^4 AR^4 \right)\]

calc_Nxx_crit() evaluates it for the given numbers of half-waves, and searches the critical one, from 1 to 10, in the direction given as None; usually n = 1 and m = None.

Shear#

For a long plate of width \(a\), simply supported on its long edges, under the shear load \(N_{xy}\), the mode of Kassapoglou [1], Eq. 6.20,

(3)#\[w = w_o \sin\frac{\pi x}{a} \sin\frac{\pi (y - x \tan\alpha)}{L}\]

has nodal lines inclined by \(\alpha\) and separated by \(L\), such that the length of the plate does not enter. Minimizing the energy with respect to \(w_o\) gives the load, Eq. 6.28, with \(AR = a/L\):

(4)#\[N_{xy,crit} = \frac{\pi^2}{2 AR^2 a^2 \tan\alpha} \left( D_{11} (1 + 6 \tan^2\alpha AR^2 + \tan^4\alpha AR^4) + 2 (D_{12} + 2 D_{66}) (AR^2 + AR^4 \tan^2\alpha) + D_{22} AR^4 \right)\]

and minimizing it with respect to \(AR\) and \(\alpha\) gives Eqs. 6.29 and 6.30:

(5)#\[\begin{split}AR = \left( \frac{D_{11}}{D_{11} \tan^4\alpha + 2 (D_{12} + 2 D_{66}) \tan^2\alpha + D_{22}} \right)^{1/4} \\ 3 D_{11} AR^4 \tan^4\alpha + (6 D_{11} AR^2 + 2 (D_{12} + 2 D_{66}) AR^4) \tan^2\alpha - (D_{11} + 2 (D_{12} + 2 D_{66}) AR^2 + D_{22} AR^4) = 0\end{split}\]

which calc_Nxy_crit() solves by Newton-Raphson iterations in \(\alpha\), starting from \(\pi/6\), with \(AR\) updated from the first equation at each iteration; RuntimeError is raised without convergence in max_iter iterations. The argument D16 is not used, and the sign of \(N_{xy}\) does not matter for \(D_{16} = D_{26} = 0\).

Combined compression and shear#

For a plate simply supported on four edges under compression \(N_o = -N_x\) and shear, Kassapoglou [1], Sec. 6.5, uses the two-term deflection of Eq. 6.31, \(w = w_1 \sin(\pi x/a) \sin(\pi y/b) + w_2 \sin(2 \pi x/a) \sin(2 \pi y/b)\), in the energy of Eq. (1). The stationarity with respect to \(w_1\) and \(w_2\) gives the eigenvalue problem \(([a] - [b]) \{w_1, w_2\} = 0\), with

(6)#\[\begin{split}a_{11} = \frac{\pi^4 D_{11} b}{4 a^3} + \frac{\pi^4 D_{12}}{2 a b} + \frac{\pi^4 D_{22} a}{4 b^3} + \frac{\pi^4 D_{66}}{a b}, \quad a_{12} = a_{21} = -\frac{160 \pi^2 D_{16}}{9 a^2} - \frac{160 \pi^2 D_{26}}{9 b^2} \\ a_{22} = \frac{4 \pi^4 D_{11} b}{a^3} + \frac{8 \pi^4 D_{12}}{a b} + \frac{4 \pi^4 D_{22} a}{b^3} + \frac{16 \pi^4 D_{66}}{a b} \\ b_{11} = \frac{\pi^2 N_o b}{4 a}, \quad b_{22} = \frac{\pi^2 N_o b}{a}, \quad b_{12} = b_{21} = \frac{32 N_{xy}}{9}\end{split}\]

whose determinant is a quadratic equation in \(N_o\); the smaller root in absolute value is returned. Two functions solve it:

  • calc_Nxx_crit_combined_shear(), the closed form of Kassapoglou, Eq. 6.34, p. 142, with \(D_{16} = D_{26} = 0\) and the shear given as the ratio \(k = N_{xy}/N_x\), such that the shear load grows with the compression:

    (7)#\[N_{xx,crit} = \frac{\pi^2}{a^2} \frac{D_{11} + 2 (D_{12} + 2 D_{66}) a^2/b^2 + D_{22} a^4/b^4}{2 - \frac{8192}{81} \frac{a^2 k^2}{b^2 \pi^4}} \left( 5 \pm \sqrt{9 + \frac{65536}{81} \frac{a^2 k^2}{b^2 \pi^4}} \right)\]

    which reduces to Eq. (2) with \(m = n = 1\) for \(k = 0\);

  • calc_Nxx_crit_combined_shear_full(), with \(D_{16}\) and \(D_{26}\) and a fixed shear load Nxy, positive in tension as in Eq. (1). With \(D_{16} = D_{26} = 0\) only \(N_{xy}^2\) enters and the result is that of the closed form for \(N_{xy} = k N_o\); otherwise the sign of the shear load changes the buckling load.

Post-buckling: effective width#

Beyond buckling, the compression \(N_{xx}\) concentrates at the supported edges. The effective width \(b_{eff}\), defined by \(\int_y N_{xx} \, dy = 2 N_{x,max} b_{eff}\), is given by Kassapoglou [1], Eq. 7.15, p. 164:

(8)#\[b_{eff} = \frac{b}{2 \left( 1 + 2 \left( 1 + \frac{A_{12}}{A_{11}} \right) \left( 1 - \frac{P_{cr}}{P_x} \right) \frac{A_{11}}{A_{11} + 3 A_{22}} \right)}\]

computed by calc_beff() from the membrane stiffnesses and the ratio of the buckling load to the applied load. At \(P_x = P_{cr}\) the whole width is effective, \(b_{eff} = b/2\), and the expression is meant for \(P_x \ge P_{cr}\).

Choice and limits#

The functions give the first estimate of a design: calc_Nxx_crit() for compression, calc_Nxy_crit() for shear of long plates, calc_Nxx_crit_combined_shear() for proportional compression and shear, calc_Nxx_crit_combined_shear_full() when the bending-twisting coupling or a fixed shear load matters, and calc_beff() for the post-buckling strength. They assume simply supported edges, a symmetric laminate and thin plates, without transverse shear deformation, which overestimates the buckling loads of thick plates and sandwiches; clamped edges, other loads, unsymmetric laminates or shear-deformable plates require a Ritz or finite element model with the stiffnesses of Laminate stiffness and plate theories.

Verification#

tests/test_kassapoglou.py compares the functions with the curves of Figs. 6.4, 6.11, 6.14 and 7.10 of Kassapoglou [1], read from the vector graphics of the book, and the sign convention of Nxy with the energy of Eq. (1), whose Hessian, integrated numerically, is singular at the returned load.
