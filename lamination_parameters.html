Lamination parameters and double-double laminates#

The ply stiffnesses are linear in \(\cos 2\theta\), \(\sin 2\theta\), \(\cos 4\theta\) and \(\sin 4\theta\), with the material invariants as coefficients, Eq. (8). For a laminate of a single material, the thickness integrals of the trigonometric terms, the lamination parameters, therefore describe the stacking sequence completely as far as the stiffness is concerned: a few continuous variables replace the ply angles, and the stiffnesses are linear in them. This is the basis of the design of laminates with lamination parameters, of their gradients for gradient-based optimization, and of the double-double and N-double laminates, whose stiffnesses are averages over a few angles.

Definition#

With \(\bar{z} = z/h\), \(z\) measured from the reference surface, and \(\theta(\bar{z})\) the ply angle, Laminate.calc_lamination_parameters() computes, exactly ply by ply, the 14 parameters stored in a LaminationParameters:

(1)#\[\begin{split}\{\xi_{A1}, \xi_{A2}, \xi_{A3}, \xi_{A4}\} = \int \{\cos 2\theta, \sin 2\theta, \cos 4\theta, \sin 4\theta\} \, d\bar{z} \\ \{\xi_{B1}, \xi_{B2}, \xi_{B3}, \xi_{B4}\} = 4 \int \{\cos 2\theta, \sin 2\theta, \cos 4\theta, \sin 4\theta\} \, \bar{z} \, d\bar{z} \\ \{\xi_{D1}, \xi_{D2}, \xi_{D3}, \xi_{D4}\} = 12 \int \{\cos 2\theta, \sin 2\theta, \cos 4\theta, \sin 4\theta\} \, \bar{z}^2 \, d\bar{z} \\ \{\xi_{A_{ts}1}, \xi_{A_{ts}2}\} = \int \{\cos 2\theta, \sin 2\theta\} \, d\bar{z}\end{split}\]

the integrals running over the thickness, \(\bar{e} - 1/2 \le \bar{z} \le \bar{e} + 1/2\), \(\bar{e} = d/h\) with \(d\) = offset. The \(\xi_{Ai}\) and \(\xi_{Di}\) are those of Krupa et al. [1], who integrate about the mid-surface; the \(\xi_{Bi}\) and the transverse shear parameters follow the same pattern, and \(\xi_{A_{ts}i} = \xi_{Ai}\). The factors 1, 4 and 12 make the integrals of a constant equal to 1, \(4\bar{e}\) and \(1 + 12\bar{e}^2\), i.e. 1, 0 and 1 about the mid-surface, where \(|\xi_{Ai}| \le 1\) and \(|\xi_{Di}| \le 1\). The \(\xi_{Ai}\) do not depend on the reference surface, whereas

(2)#\[\xi_{Bi} = \xi_{Bi}^0 + 4 \bar{e} \xi_{Ai}, \qquad \xi_{Di} = \xi_{Di}^0 + 6 \bar{e} \xi_{Bi}^0 + 12 \bar{e}^2 \xi_{Ai}\]

with the superscript 0 for the parameters about the mid-surface, the counterpart of Eq. (7). Parameters with an offset may therefore lie outside the feasible region of the parameters about the mid-surface.

Stiffness from lamination parameters#

Integrating Eq. (8) through the thickness with \(\int dz = h \int d\bar{z}\), \(\int z \, dz = h^2/4 \cdot 4 \int \bar{z} \, d\bar{z}\) and \(\int z^2 dz = h^3/12 \cdot 12 \int \bar{z}^2 d\bar{z}\) gives the stiffnesses of laminate_from_LaminationParameters():

(3)#\[\begin{split}A_{11} = h (u_1 + u_2 \xi_{A1} + u_3 \xi_{A3}), \quad A_{12} = h (u_4 - u_3 \xi_{A3}), \quad A_{22} = h (u_1 - u_2 \xi_{A1} + u_3 \xi_{A3}) \\ A_{16} = h \left( \frac{u_2}{2} \xi_{A2} + u_3 \xi_{A4} \right), \quad A_{26} = h \left( \frac{u_2}{2} \xi_{A2} - u_3 \xi_{A4} \right), \quad A_{66} = h (u_5 - u_3 \xi_{A3}) \\ B_{11} = \frac{h^2}{4} (4\bar{e} u_1 + u_2 \xi_{B1} + u_3 \xi_{B3}), \quad \dots, \qquad D_{11} = \frac{h^3}{12} ((1 + 12\bar{e}^2) u_1 + u_2 \xi_{D1} + u_3 \xi_{D3}), \quad \dots \\ \bar{A}_{44} = h (u_6 + u_7 \xi_{A_{ts}1}), \quad \bar{A}_{45} = -h u_7 \xi_{A_{ts}2}, \quad \bar{A}_{55} = h (u_6 - u_7 \xi_{A_{ts}1})\end{split}\]

where \(B_{ij}\) and \(D_{ij}\) follow the pattern of \(A_{ij}\) with the parameters \(\xi_{Bi}\) and \(\xi_{Di}\), and with the constant terms \(u_1\), \(u_4\), \(u_5\) multiplied by \(4\bar{e}\) and \(1 + 12\bar{e}^2\), respectively. laminate_from_lamination_parameters() does the same from the 14 values given as arguments. With the default plane-stress invariants of MatLamina.rebuild(), and the same offset, these are exactly the stiffnesses of laminated_plate() for the layup that gave the parameters (Jones [2], Eq. (4.24)). The parameters \(\xi_{A_{ts}i}\) multiply \(u_7 = (G_{23} - G_{13})/2\), and only matter when \(G_{13} \ne G_{23}\).

The lamination parameters do not describe the through-thickness distribution of the plies, such that a laminate created from them has no plies and only \(A\), \(B\), \(D\) and \(\bar{A}_{ts}\): \(E\), \(F\), \(H\), \(D_{ts}\), \(F_{ts}\) and the mass integrals are zero, \(\bar{\bar{A}}_{ts}\) is nan, no shear correction can be computed, shear_correction is None and Ats = Abar_ts, see Transverse shear stiffness and shear correction. Such a laminate serves the CLPT and, with a user factor on Abar_ts, the FSDT.

Special laminates#

The functions make_balanced_LP(), make_symmetric_LP() and make_orthotropic_LP() set to zero, in place, the parameters that vanish in the respective laminates, consistently with the methods of Laminate stiffness and plate theories:

  • balanced, a ply at \(-\theta\) for every ply at \(+\theta\): the integrals of \(\sin 2\theta\) and \(\sin 4\theta\) without weight, \(\xi_{A2}\), \(\xi_{A4}\), \(\xi_{A_{ts}2}\), whereas \(\xi_{B2}\), \(\xi_{B4}\), \(\xi_{D2}\), \(\xi_{D4}\) depend on the positions of the plies;

  • symmetric about the mid-surface: \(\xi_{Bi} = 0\), which holds for the parameters about the mid-surface only; with an offset \(\xi_{Bi} = 4\bar{e} \xi_{Ai}\) in a symmetric laminate, Eq. (2);

  • orthotropic, all plies at 0 or 90 degrees: all the sine parameters. The \(\xi_{D2}\) and \(\xi_{D4}\), related to the bending-twisting coupling, become small in balanced laminates with many plies.

Gradients#

At constant thickness the stiffnesses of Eq. (3) are linear in the lamination parameters, with gradients that depend only on the thickness and on the material, and at constant lamination parameters and offset they are polynomials of \(h\). GradABD.calc_LP_grad() stores both in the attributes of GradABD: gradAij, gradBij and gradDij, of shape (6, 5), rows 11, 12, 16, 22, 26, 66, column 0 the derivative with respect to \(h\) and columns 1 to 4 those with respect to the four parameters of the matrix, and gradAtsij, of shape (3, 3), rows Abar44, Abar45, Abar55, columns \(h\), \(\xi_{A_{ts}1}\), \(\xi_{A_{ts}2}\). With \(U\) the rows of the in-plane terms of MatLamina.get_invariant_matrix(), \(A = h \, U \{1, \xi_A\}\), \(B = h d \, U \{1, 0\} + h^2/4 \, U \{0, \xi_B\}\) and \(D = (h^3/12 + h d^2) \, U \{1, 0\} + h^3/12 \, U \{0, \xi_D\}\), such that

(4)#\[\frac{\partial A}{\partial h} = U \{1, \xi_A\}, \quad \frac{\partial B}{\partial h} = d \, U \{1, 0\} + \frac{h}{2} U \{0, \xi_B\}, \quad \frac{\partial D}{\partial h} = \left( \frac{h^2}{4} + d^2 \right) U \{1, 0\} + \frac{h^2}{4} U \{0, \xi_D\}\]

and the derivatives with respect to the parameters are the columns of \(U\) times \(h\), \(h^2/4\) and \(h^3/12\); analogously for \(\bar{A}_{ts}\). The transverse shear gradients are those of the uncorrected \(\bar{A}_{ts}\), the Ats of the lamination-parameter route.

Double-double and N-double laminates#

A double-double laminate is the repetition of the sub-laminate \([\pm\phi, \pm\psi]\), and an N-double laminate that of \([\pm\phi_1, \pm\phi_2, \cdots, \pm\phi_n]\), all plies of the same material and thickness (Shrivastava et al. [3]). In the limit of many repetitions the laminate is homogenized: \(B = 0\), and the stiffnesses are the averages of the ply stiffnesses over the \(2n\) angles. They are written with the trace \(\mathrm{tr}\) and the trace-normalized invariants \(u_i^*\) of MatLamina.trace_normalize_plane_stress() (Melo et al. [4]):

(5)#\[\begin{split}A_{11}^* = u_1^* + \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( u_2^* \cos 2\phi_i + u_3^* \cos 4\phi_i \right), \quad A_{22}^* = u_1^* + \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( -u_2^* \cos 2\phi_i + u_3^* \cos 4\phi_i \right) \\ A_{12}^* = u_4^* - \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} u_3^* \cos 4\phi_i, \quad A_{66}^* = u_5^* - \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} u_3^* \cos 4\phi_i, \quad A_{16}^* = A_{26}^* = 0 \\ A_{ij} = \mathrm{tr} \, A_{ij}^* h, \qquad D_{ij} = \mathrm{tr} \, A_{ij}^* h^3/12\end{split}\]

the bending stiffnesses being those of the smeared laminate of Laminate.make_smeared(). For the double-double laminate, with \(\alpha = (\phi + \psi)/2\) and \(\beta = (\phi - \psi)/2\), the averages are \(\cos 2\alpha \cos 2\beta\) and \(\cos 4\alpha \cos 4\beta\), which double_double_laminate() uses. The three functions give the same stiffnesses:

  • double_double_laminate(), from laminaprop and the two angles;

  • composites.utils.n_double_laminate(), from laminaprop and a list of angles;

  • composites.core.n_double_laminate(), the compiled version, from a MatLamina, which it trace-normalizes in place, and the first n angles of an array.

The laminates have no plies: only \(A\) and \(D\) are computed, all the other stiffnesses, the transverse shear ones included, are zero, as are the mass integrals, and the equivalent properties of Laminate.calc_equivalent_properties() are computed. They serve the CLPT, with the homogenization assumption; an FSDT or TSDT model needs the stiffnesses of a stacking sequence, e.g. from laminated_plate().

Verification#

tests/test_core.py and tests/test_plane_stress_invariants.py check that the lamination parameters of a laminate give back its \(A\), \(B\), \(D\) and \(\bar{A}_{ts}\) through laminate_from_LaminationParameters(), also with an offset and with three-dimensional constants, against a classical-lamination-theory implementation of the tests and the shift of Vasiliev and Morozov [5], Eq. (5.28); the special-laminate functions; and the gradients of GradABD against central differences. tests/test_DD_laminate.py, tests/test_ND_laminate.py and tests/test_ND_laminate_core.py check the trace against Melo et al. [4], Table 1, and the \(A\), \(D\) of the double-double and N-double laminates against those of laminated_plate() with 32 repetitions of the sub-laminate.
